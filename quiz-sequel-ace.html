Learn the ones you missed

Keysi shows every shortcut of the app you're in when you hold ⌘, and Practice drills the ones you keep missing until they stick. One drill a day is free. Keysi Pro adds the Coach, which notices when you reach for the mouse and tells you the keys instead.

Practice in Keysi Download Keysi free

“Practice in Keysi” opens Keysi's Practice window if Keysi is installed. Without Keysi, your browser will say it can't open the link.
