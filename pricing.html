Keysi pricing

Learn Mac shortcuts without a subscription.

The complete shortcut reference is free. Keysi Pro is the optional learning loop: one payment for reminders, practice and progress that help the shortcuts you keep missing become automatic.

Free: the complete shortcut reference

The free app has no trial, time limit, account or card requirement. Hold ⌘, ⌥, ⌃ or ⇧ to see the frontmost Mac app's live menu shortcuts, search them, run a command from a row, and export the list as Markdown or a printable PDF.

  • Search, favourites, hiding and click-to-run shortcuts.
  • Thirty-five built-in cheat sheets plus custom sheets and an editor.
  • Warnings when macOS or another app takes a shortcut first.
  • Raycast, Alfred, terminal and keysi:// access to the free viewer.
  • One Practice drill a day to try spaced repetition.

Keysi Pro: $14.99 once

Every install includes 14 days of Pro, followed by a short 7-day grace period. When that ends, Keysi keeps working as the free reference; your local setup and learning data are not deleted.

  • Coach: a quiet reminder when you use a menu command that has a shortcut.
  • Practice: unlimited spaced-repetition drills for the shortcuts you keep missing.
  • Learning plans: learn one app's shortcuts a few at a time.
  • Progress: weekly goals, per-app mastery and locally measured menu-trip improvements.
  • Integrations: Practice and Progress from Raycast; Practice and export from Alfred; Practice, Progress and export from Shortcuts and the terminal.
  • Desktop-picture export: make any app's shortcuts a wallpaper-sized reference.
  • Three Macs: move the license between up to three Macs.
  • Free updates and a 30-day refund: Gumroad handles checkout and VAT.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing stops working. The live shortcut overlay, search, custom sheets, exports, integrations that open the free viewer and one daily Practice drill remain available. Pro-only Coach reminders, unlimited Practice, learning plans and progress pause until you activate a license.

Why is Keysi not a subscription?

Keysi is sold as a one-time purchase because its core work happens on your Mac: reading menus, showing shortcuts and keeping your learning history locally. There is no hosted account to maintain, and updates to the app are free.
