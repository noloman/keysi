How to add your voice

I want this too opens Keysi's own feedback form with that request already selected. Add a line about how you would use it if you like. The form shows exactly what will be sent, and nothing is sent until you press Send. There is no account and no sign-in.

The button needs Keysi 1.0.15 or later installed on this Mac. Without it, your browser will say it can't open the link. Something that isn't listed? Use Send Feedback… in Keysi's menu bar.
