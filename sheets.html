Shortcuts that aren't in the menu bar

Keysi reads an app's live menus, so it can show every shortcut of the app you're in without anyone maintaining a list. Some apps get around that: Vim and tmux have no macOS menus, and Electron apps like Slack keep their real bindings out of the menu bar.

For those, Keysi ships hand-written sheets. They're free, not part of Keysi Pro, and you can fork any of them.

For apps whose shortcuts are in the menu bar, see the shortcut lists by app, read straight from those menus.

Write your own

Any app can have one. A sheet is a JSON file in ~/Library/Application Support/Keysi/Sheets/ naming the app and the shortcuts to list, and Keysi's built-in editor can write it for you. Set mode to augment to add to what Keysi found in the app's menus, or replace to drop that, which is what you want for an Electron app whose menu bar doesn't match its real bindings.

Download any sheet above as a starting point.

Custom sheets are free. So is the editor, and everything else on this page.
