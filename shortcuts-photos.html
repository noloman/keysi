This page is a snapshot. Photos changes its menus between versions, and some of its shortcuts only appear for whatever is selected. Keysi reads the menus of the Photos you are actually running: hold ⌘ and every shortcut shows up over the app, grouped like this, and clicking a row runs it.

That part of Keysi is free, with no time limit.
