Your license is ready.

Gumroad sends your license key in the purchase receipt. Download Keysi, open its license settings, then paste the key to turn Pro on.

Open Keysi license settings Download Keysi for Mac
  1. Find the key. Open the receipt email from Gumroad and copy the license key.
  2. Open Keysi. If it is not installed yet, download it above and grant Accessibility permission when macOS asks.
  3. Activate. Use “Open Keysi license settings”, or open Keysi → Settings → Keysi Pro yourself.
  4. Paste and go. Paste the key, choose Activate, and start with one Coach reminder or Practice drill.

If the first button does nothing, Keysi is not installed or is not running. Open the app once, then use the button again. Your key stays in the Gumroad receipt; Keysi does not send it to this website.
