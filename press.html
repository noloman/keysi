Keysi, for writers and reviewers

At a glance

  • Name: Keysi
  • One-liner: Hold ⌘ for a moment and see every keyboard shortcut of the app you're using, grouped by menu, with real key glyphs, click any row to run it.
  • Platform: macOS 14 or later
  • Price: the app is free, with no time limit, no account or card required. Keysi Pro is $19.99, one-time: the coaching (Shortcut Coach, Practice Mode, measured progress, a weekly summary, progress marks on the overlay), export (Markdown, a printable PDF, a desktop picture) and the integrations (Raycast, Alfred, Shortcuts, Spotlight and Siri, a Services entry, the keysi terminal command, keysi:// URLs). Every install starts with 14 days of Pro, and when they end the app keeps working as the free tier.
  • Languages: 9: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese
  • Distribution: direct download, signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple, and not on the Mac App Store
  • Developer: Manuel Lorenzo

Description (100 words)

Keysi is a macOS menu-bar utility that reveals and runs the keyboard shortcuts of whatever app is frontmost. Hold ⌘ for a glance, or press a hotkey for a searchable panel. It reads the app's real menu structure live via the Accessibility API, so every shortcut is accurate to that app in that state, dimmed when unavailable and flagged when macOS intercepts it first. Coach mode notices when you pick a menu command that already has a shortcut, and offers one quiet reminder. Nine languages. An independent recreation of CheatSheet, discontinued on Sonoma. The app is free; $19.99 once adds coaching, export and integrations.
