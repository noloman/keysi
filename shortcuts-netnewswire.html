This page is a snapshot. NetNewsWire changes its menus between versions, and some of its shortcuts only appear for whatever is selected. Keysi reads the menus of the NetNewsWire you are actually running: hold ⌘ and every shortcut shows up over the app, grouped like this, and clicking a row runs it.

That part of Keysi is free, with no time limit. Remembering them is a different problem: how to learn a Mac app's shortcuts.
