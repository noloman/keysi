Google Sheets keyboard shortcuts on macOS

Google Sheets runs in your browser, so its shortcuts live in the page, not in any macOS menu bar. Hold ⌘ in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge or Brave while Google Sheets is open and Keysi shows them next to the browser's own.

40 shortcuts · adds to what Keysi reads from the app's own menus · free, and not part of Keysi Pro.

Use this sheet

It's already in Keysi. Install the app and hold ⌘ in Google Sheets.

Keysi lists it as Google Sheets in Settings ▸ Shortcuts.

To change it, download the file below and put it in ~/Library/Application Support/Keysi/Sheets/ (Settings ▸ Shortcuts ▸ “Reveal Sheets Folder”). A sheet with the same id replaces the built-in one, so your edits stick. Or use Keysi's editor and skip the JSON.

In a browser, matches the page that is open: docs.google.com/spreadsheets.
